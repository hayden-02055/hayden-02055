## About Me

I'm an **LLM & Backend Engineer** focused on turning AI product ideas into services that real users can test.

I work across backend architecture, LLM integration, databases, deployment, and operational workflows. Rather than overbuilding infrastructure at the beginning, I focus on creating the smallest practical product that can answer important business questions:

- Will real users actually use it?
- Does the LLM feature provide meaningful value?
- Can the service operate at a reasonable cost?
- Is the product worth improving and scaling?

Previously, I led backend engineering and technical architecture for a live consumer service as CTO. I'm currently studying Artificial Intelligence in South Korea and building the Sentinel ecosystem.
